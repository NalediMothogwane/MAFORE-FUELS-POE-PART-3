Who We Are

MAFORE FUELS, also known as MFR FUELS, is an independent petrol station founded in November 2025.

MFR FUELS serves the Tsimanyane community and neighbouring areas by providing convenient access to fuel.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality fuel, reliable service and convenience.

Our Vision

Our Vision is to become the preffered independent petrol station in the region.

Our Slogan

"FUELING Every Journey With Excellence"

Community Benefits

MFR Fuels helps community members save fuel, time and travel costs by providing a nearby fuel station.
